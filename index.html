Questions

Is Tekrid a taxi company?

No. Tekrid is a matching app, not a dispatcher or a cab fleet. It helps you find a nearby driver who's already available — you handle the ride and payment directly with them.

Who sets the fare?

Tekrid shows a reference fare (₹10 plus ₹10 per km) as a starting point so neither side is guessing. You and the driver agree on the final price together.

How are drivers verified?

Drivers submit their documents when they sign up, and each one is manually reviewed before that driver can appear in anyone's search.

Does joining the waitlist cost anything?

No. Joining the waitlist is free, and so is using Tekrid once it launches — there's no subscription or booking fee.

When does Tekrid launch?

We're building the Android app now. Everyone on the waitlist gets an email the moment Tekrid is ready in their city.
